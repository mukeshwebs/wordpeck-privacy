Wordnest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Wordnest ("the app", package com.puzzle.wordlex) is a word game published by Puzzle Buzz. This policy explains what information is used and why.

Information stored by the app

Your progress (levels, coins, stickers, settings, and the name and avatar you choose) is stored only on your device. We do not create accounts or send this progress to our servers.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose "Connect" with Google, the app receives your Google account name and email only to show your name on your profile. We do not store them outside your device.

Ads

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on IP address, and usage information to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Reset progress to delete all of your progress from the device. You can also uninstall the app.
